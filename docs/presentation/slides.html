🚨 The Raw Source Data Breakdown
  • 20,400 Raw UPI Transactions: Spaced UTRs (1,881), negative refund amounts (429), 14 status casing variants, and 400 exact row duplicates.
  • 36,400 Raw Customer KYC Records: 4,235 duplicate IDs with conflicting PANs, Aadhaars, and KYC statuses across multiple rows.
  • 6,210 Raw Merchant Masters: 1,127 duplicate IDs with conflicting bank accounts, missing MCCs, and trade name discrepancies.
  • 2,884 Raw Chargeback Logs: 219 unlinked orphan disputes and 183 empty disputed amount values.
⚡ Why Generic Pipelines Fail

The Naïve Approach: Generic pipelines either blindly drop dirty rows (distorting financial volume) or run unverified dashboards on corrupted identifiers.

The FinData Approach: We enforce Preservation over Deletion. Every anomaly is standardized, scored with resolution confidence, linked via foreign keys, and cataloged into an immutable audit diff matrix.

The Syndicate Reality: Fraudsters exploit multi-entity setups—319 shared Aadhaar clusters and 71 shared settlement bank accounts connect seemingly independent accounts into organized rings.
